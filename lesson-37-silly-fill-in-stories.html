    Eight tiny challenges to show off everything you learnt this month. Ready, set… 🔎

    6Sentences & Verbs 7Words & Figures
    6Sentences & VerbsA · B
    AFirst or second conditional?
    1.If it rains tomorrow, we will stay inside. 2.If I were a bird, I would fly away.
    BRewrite each certain sentence as a possibility — use may, might or could
    1.It will snow tonight. 2.She is at home.
    6Sentences & VerbsC · D
    CName the sentence type
    statementquestioncommandexclamation
    1.What a wonderful day this is! 2.Please close the door. 3.Where are you going?
    DWrite the correct verb so it agrees with the subject
    1.The dog (bark) loudly. 2.The dogs (bark) loudly. 3.The children (be) happy.
    7Words & FiguresA · B

    1.big → / 2.happy → / 3.good → /
    BWrite a synonym AND an antonym for each word

    💡 Listen to the sound the word starts with — not the letter!

    DHyperbole or oxymoron?
    1.I'm so hungry I could eat a hundred pizzas! 2.The room was filled with a deafening silence.
    🎒 Homework Missions
    • 1Write your own silly fill-in story (about six sentences) with blanks. Label each blank with a grammar type, then ask someone in your family to fill it in!
    • 2Write one sentence each using a first conditional, a hyperbole and an oxymoron.
    • 3Write five pairs of words that are synonyms, and five pairs that are antonyms.
